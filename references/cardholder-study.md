# Imported cardholder geometry study

The user supplied object `1776246757315` in the live Blender scene. Its original mesh and transform are preserved in `imported-cardholder.blend`, and in the live-scene checkpoint under `revisions/v4`.

Inspected front, back and near-side clay renders, and sampled front/back surface intersections at several heights. The mesh is one triangulated surface with 149,970 vertices and 299,952 triangles; it has no material slots or live construction modifiers. Consequently, its original modelling process cannot be recovered directly.

Useful visible construction features:

- The main panel is broadly flat. Most curvature is concentrated in a soft transition towards the stitched perimeter.
- The stitched edge is compressed relative to the panel face; the thread sits in this margin instead of floating above an undifferentiated plane.
- Pocket openings have a rounded lip with a narrow recessed reveal. Their thickness is visible in silhouette.
- The back is much flatter than the front; layer thickness accumulates towards the face.
- The outer binding stays within the backing silhouette. Pocket lips meet that edge without a protruding side bar.

At a proportional width of 105 mm, sampled lower-panel thickness is roughly 4–7 mm, depending on height and distance from the seam. These are approximate comparisons: the imported object's aspect ratio, tilt and pocket layout differ from the CEOMENTALITY design.

Applied to the final v4: compressed sewn borders, thin independent pocket skins, small edge-tension dents, soft creases and varied polygonal threads. The side filler and open punctures were removed following the user's later corrections. Bottom stitches overlap at an angle; side and top stitches remain aligned with their seam. Pocket mouths remain unstitched. The final lower radius is 7 mm, upper corners are nearly square, and the total depth is approximately 3.57 mm. Original reference mesh and lettering remain separate from the delivered asset.
